32. **RS256 is the cheapest of the three JWT signature algorithms to VERIFY on this CPU** (OpenSSL 3.0.13, one thread pinned to cpu 2, three runs, spread under 1%): RSA-2048 verify about 76,900/s (13 microseconds), ECDSA P-256 verify about 21,150/s (47 us), Ed25519 verify about 12,000-12,200/s (82-84 us); signing is the reverse (RSA-2048 about 2,180/s, ECDSA about 62,400/s, Ed25519 about 32,700/s).
